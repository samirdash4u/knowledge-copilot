# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to team needs. Employees in client-facing branch roles are not eligible for regular remote work.

## Home office stipend

Eligible employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 to purchase a desk, chair or monitor. The stipend is claimed through the expense system with receipts.

## Security requirements

All remote work must be performed on a company-managed laptop. The corporate VPN must be connected whenever accessing internal systems. Working from public Wi-Fi without the VPN is prohibited.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Working from abroad requires written approval from both the employee's manager and the HR department, because of tax and data residency rules.
