# Code of Conduct

## Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts from clients or suppliers with a value under $100. Any gift of $100 or more must be declined or reported to Compliance within 5 business days. Cash gifts are never permitted.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose any outside business interest, directorship or family relationship that could influence their decisions. Disclosures are made annually in the compliance portal and whenever circumstances change.

## Speaking up

Concerns about misconduct can be reported to your manager, to Compliance, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline, which is available 24 hours a day. Acme Financial prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
