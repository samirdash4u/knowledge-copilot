# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Acme Financial. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per month.

Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Requesting leave

Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences of more than 3 days.

## Approving leave
<!-- acl: manager -->

Managers must approve or decline a leave request within 3 business days. If a manager does not respond, the request is escalated to the manager's own manager automatically. Managers may decline leave during the quarter-end close period (the last 5 business days of each quarter) for finance teams. Internal reference: CANARY-MGR-LEAVE-41A.
