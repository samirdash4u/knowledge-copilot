# Travel and Expense Policy

## Meals

The daily meal allowance while travelling is $75 for domestic travel and $100 for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Receipts and deadlines

Itemised receipts are required for every expense over $25. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted later than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Air travel

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.

## Approval limits
<!-- acl: manager -->

Managers can approve individual expense claims up to $5,000. Claims above $5,000 require director approval, and claims above $25,000 require CFO approval. Managers must not approve their own expenses. Internal reference: CANARY-MGR-EXP-7C2.
