# Security Incident Response Policy

## Reporting an incident

Every employee must report a suspected security incident within 1 hour of noticing it, by emailing security@acme.example or calling the security hotline. Do not try to investigate the incident yourself.

## Severity levels

A Sev1 incident is any confirmed breach of Restricted or Confidential data, or an outage of customer-facing payment systems. Sev1 incidents page the on-call security lead immediately.

## Regulator notification
<!-- acl: admin -->

Personal data breaches must be notified to the supervisory authority within 72 hours of discovery. Only the Chief Information Security Officer may authorise a regulator notification. Internal reference: CANARY-ADM-IR-6D1.
