# Privileged Access Management Standard

## Access reviews

Privileged access to production systems is reviewed quarterly by system owners. Access not re-certified within 14 days of a review is revoked automatically.

## Break-glass accounts

Break-glass accounts are stored in the password vault and may only be used during a declared Sev1 incident. Every use must be reviewed by the security team within 24 hours. Internal reference: CANARY-ADM-PAM-2A4.
