# Disciplinary Procedure

## Steps

The disciplinary process has four stages: a verbal warning, a written warning, a performance improvement plan (PIP), and termination. A PIP lasts 60 days. Termination may only proceed after consultation with HR Employee Relations.

## Gross misconduct

Fraud, theft and violence are gross misconduct and may lead to immediate suspension on full pay pending investigation. Internal reference: CANARY-MGR-DISC-3B7.
