# On-call Handbook

## Rotation

On-call rotations are one week long and hand over every Monday at 10:00. Each rotation has a primary and a secondary engineer.

## Responding to pages

Acknowledge a page within 5 minutes. If the primary has not acknowledged within 15 minutes, the page escalates to the secondary, and then to the engineering manager.

## On-call compensation
<!-- acl: manager -->

Engineers receive an on-call allowance of $300 per week of primary on-call, plus time off in lieu for pages handled between midnight and 6am. Managers approve allowances in the payroll system. Internal reference: CANARY-MGR-ONCALL-9E3.
